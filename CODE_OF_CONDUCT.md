# Code of Conduct

ReelMind is a friendly place to learn, build and share. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
- Assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and keep discussions on topic.
- Respect creators: don't share or ask for other people's downloaded videos, accounts or private data.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, ethnicity, nationality, age, disability, sexual orientation or appearance.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as addresses, phone numbers or API keys, without permission.
- Spam, or promotion unrelated to the discussion.

## Where this applies

These rules apply in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and code review), and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting a problem

Contact the maintainer, [@sisi-tarak](https://github.com/sisi-tarak), privately on GitHub. For anything involving security or leaked keys, use the private report described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and may:
- warn the person
- remove the comment, issue or pull request
- block them from the project, temporarily or permanently

The aim is a safe, welcoming project, not punishment.
